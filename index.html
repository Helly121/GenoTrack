Rice
Genotype

Explore rice accessions represented by genomic marker profiles. Genotype data captures inherited variation that can help explain differences in grain yield and other breeding traits.

GenoTrack uses SNP marker information alongside trial-environment data to estimate performance. Select Breeding Lab to choose an accession and environment, run a prediction, and inspect its explanations.

Field
Phenotype

Phenotypes are the traits observed in trials, such as grain yield. Their values reflect both inherited differences and the conditions in which each accession is grown, so accession, season, and trial records must be interpreted together.

G×E
Model

Genotype-by-environment (G×E) modelling estimates how genetic performance may change across growing conditions. GenoTrack combines genomic signals and environmental context; component decomposition and SHAP attribution help inspect which factors contribute to a prediction.

Yield
Prediction

Review the model's reported validation metrics, then open the Breeding Lab to predict grain yield for a selected rice accession and target environment. Predictions are model estimates and should be interpreted alongside validation results.
